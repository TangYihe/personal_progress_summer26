## Daily Log

### 2026-08-03

**PLAN CHANGE: the workstation travels to the filming site.** On-site provisioning is retired; the
problem becomes transport + re-cabling. Documented tomorrow's pre-pack verification, the shoot-script
risk register, and the props/site requirements.

- **What the change retires.** The whole provisioning thread existed to hedge against a machine we
  had never seen: the offline nix install (and the never-captured-tarball bug), the two robo-nix revs
  that both had to resolve offline via `~/.cache/nix`, the venv-rebuild fallback, and — the scariest
  one, because it was unanswerable remotely — **whether the TIANJI box was NVIDIA**, since
  `robo.nix` hardcodes `hostGraphics = "nixgl-nvidia"` and that gates the MuJoCo viewer. All moot.
  The **dirty working tree** travels too, which removes a real fragility: our LOCAL uncommitted edits
  (`wuji_finetune.json`, `pico_world_wuji_hands.yaml`, `driver_supervisor.py`) live on no branch, and
  the seed only carried them by copying the tree.
- **The upside that wasn't in the old plan: the full deploy stack comes with us.** The seed was
  deliberately **teleop-only** (training venvs and policies dropped). With the workstation on-site,
  the validated 16/16 cube-twist policy and `we deploy` are available → **autonomous shots become a
  real option for the script**, not just teleoperated ones.
- **What it does *not* fix — the risk changed shape, from software portability to physical
  transport, and there is now no fallback box:**
  - **The NIC card.** Both ethernet ports (`enp13s0` teleop LAN, `enp14s0` robot) are on a single
    add-in card. Transport shock is exactly what unseats it → if both links misbehave on-site,
    **reseat the card first**, before debugging cables or config.
  - **Both networks get rebuilt by hand** on-site (see the IP analysis below).
  - **Everything gets replugged**, so every replug lesson reapplies: cross-wired glove cables
    (07-27), the D455 silently dropping to USB2 on an under-seated USB-A plug, the **marked** A→C
    converter orientation. Check `/sys/bus/usb/devices/*/speed` before trusting anything.
  - **The robot is transported too** → expect an init-gate trip on first bring-up (recover via
    上位机), and re-verify the charger on arrival.
  - **A display, keyboard and mouse are now required on-site** — the MuJoCo viewer needs a real
    display. Folded into the props/site asks.
- **IP addressing after the move — two of three are guaranteed:**
  - ✅ **Robot ↔ workstation unchanged.** `enp14s0` = static `6.6.7.166`, direct cable; robot x86
    `6.6.7.190`, Orin `6.6.7.100`, chassis `6.6.7.6`. No router in this path.
  - ✅ **Gloves unchanged.** Two USB-ethernet dongles, static `.166/.167` + host routes, gloves at
    `.100/.101`. Direct, no router. The transport risk here is **cross-wiring**, not addressing.
  - ⚠️ **`enp13s0` = `192.168.31.48` is NOT guaranteed — it is DHCP** from the Xiaomi router.
    Bringing the router preserves the `192.168.31.0/24` subnet and probably the lease, but an
    expired lease, a cleared lease table, or another device claiming `.48` first would change it —
    and `192.168.31.48` is hardcoded both in `export TELEOP_XROBOT_HOST_IP=…` and in the headset
    app's connect target. The failure mode presents as *"teleop doesn't track"*, which is a bad
    thing to debug on a film set. **→ Lock it static before packing** (the previously deprioritized
    TODO, now promoted; command in the pre-pack list above).
  - ℹ️ **No internet on-site is still fine, but stay frozen.** With no VPN/uplink, entering a
    `robo shell` must not need to fetch anything. **Do not `git pull` or touch `flake.lock` on-site**
    — that is precisely what broke the Orin on 07-29 (`Could not resolve host` after refactor-0722
    bumped the robo-nix pin). The `~/.cache/nix` eval-cache lesson survives the plan change.
- **Workstation availability is a non-issue** — Yihe is the only user of that machine (confirmed
  08-03), so removing it from the lab for the shoot window costs nothing. ⚠️ It does mean **no
  training runs** while it's away.
