- **`provision.sh` (on the seed) automates all of Part B and self-checks** — it proves the box is

- **🔌 USB gotcha (~25 min lost):** the 双接口 stick enumerated at **480 Mb/s / `version 2.10`** on its
  **USB-A** end even in a blue USB3 port — on A plugs the SuperSpeed pins are recessed, so a slightly
  unseated plug silently runs at USB2. **Type-C end → 5000 Mb/s / `version 3.20`.** Check
  `/sys/bus/usb/devices/<n>/speed` before trusting a transfer.
- **Stick throughput is wildly asymmetric — write ~2–6 MB/s, read ~150 MB/s.** rsync's
  `--info=progress2` reported `11.30MB/s` and appeared to hang at 99%: the kernel was buffering into
  page cache and the real flash write happened in the trailing `sync` (`D` state at
  `wb_wait_for_completion`, ~1 GB dirty draining). **Don't unplug before `sync` returns.** Because
  *reads* are 150 MB/s, **no drive purchase is needed** for provisioning (an earlier
  buy-an-SSD recommendation was retracted once measured). A bigger SSD is still worth considering
  purely to haul recorded episodes home from the shoot.
- ℹ️ Also learned while reading `--info=progress2`: the 4th column is **elapsed**, not ETA, and the
  `%` is untrustworthy under incremental recursion — `ir-chk=A/B` (→ `to-chk` once enumeration
  finishes) is the honest progress signal.
