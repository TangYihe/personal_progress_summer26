## Slide 2 — Literature: Dexterity from human video

**Two lines of work found:**

**Line 1 — Human video, simpler tasks** (DexWild, Ego-Pi / π₀.₅):
- Tasks are relatively simple → motion migration is less challenging.
- All these works deliberately match viewpoints between human and robot
  (wrist-mounted cameras at same placement, egocentric head view).
- **Takeaway for us:** matching human ↔ robot visual observations is a confirmed design choice.

**Line 2 — Contact-rich, harder tasks** (DexMachina, ManipTrans):
- Target dexterous, high-contact tasks.
- Both use state-based RL to fix poor retargeting (show task videos here).
- Both rely on sim for the RL step — **not directly portable to our sim-free setting.**
- **Cross-paper consensus:** direct kinematic retargeting from human video yields very low
  success rate on contact-rich tasks.

**Derisk objective this creates:**
We must empirically test human video replay on our tasks — quantify the retargeting gap
before committing to any RL approach.

## Slide 3 — Literature: Force / torque-aware learning

**What existing works do with torque:**
- τ_ext (external joint torque) is the primary signal — available on our robot arms natively.
- Role: **contact detection and force magnitude**, not used as a policy action target.
- Placement: alongside joint positions in the policy's proprio stream
  (FACTR: appended as a "force token" in the ACT encoder; TA-VLA: 10-frame history → MLP token).
- Auxiliary prediction option: expand the action space to also predict torque, forcing the
  policy to internally model contact (TA-VLA approach).

**Intuition for how our current plan would work:**
- **Action target** = joint position targets from teleop (impedance control mode).
- **Both collection and deployment** use joint impedance controller.
- Because the target may be partially unreachable (blocked by an object), the controller
  produces a compliant force push proportional to the position error.
- τ_ext then captures this contact naturally — no extra sensor needed beyond what we already
  have.
- → Clean signal, consistent between training and deployment.
